Heads up

Gen 2 Heroes are coming

Every hero generation brings three new heroes — one Infantry, one Lancer, and one Marksman. Each generation includes 5–6 Lucky Wheels (a generation lasts about 80 days). You'll still have the option to pick previous heroes too.

Flint arrives on the wheel in three days and some hours — an Infantry hero, and an important one to get. He's a massive upgrade over Sergey.

There's no exact number of diamonds you need to get him, but the wheel comes down to luck plus specific spin milestones that guarantee hero shards.

  • 1 spin: 1,500 diamonds — 10 spins: 13,500 diamonds.
  • Use the 10-spin option — it's cheaper per spin.
  • Guaranteed shard-chest milestones will show on the wheel once the event goes live.
  • The last milestone is at 120 spins — don't spin past that unless you're seriously loaded. Reaching 120 spins takes roughly 162,000 diamonds.
  • HOC Day 2 gives points toward the wheel.

Note: don't use general shards on wheel heroes.

Summary:

  • Only spin the wheel if you have enough gems for 70 or 120 total spins per event.
  • Always spin 10x at a time to get the discount.
  • Your goal is to get at least 4 stars on the hero.
  • Never use general shards on a wheel hero — better to save them until you have ~475, then use them all on a non-wheel hero at the start of a future generation to get them to 4 stars.
  • If you participate in HOC, use the wheel on Day 2 too.

Diplomacy

NAP Agreement

Non-aggression pact negotiated between R4/R5 of both alliances. All members must follow these terms:

  1. No attacks on cities, rallies, HQ, or banners of NAP members.
  2. No scouting NAP cities or resource tiles.
  3. No poaching NAP players.
  4. Violations are solved first between R4/R5 of both alliances.
  5. Penalties hit the player only, not the alliance — 1st: warning, 2nd: demotion, 3rd: banned from NAP.
  6. Fortress / Facility / Stronghold: no city hits during these battles; 1 fortress per alliance (by rank, checked Sundays); Stronghold is Top 4 alliances only; no path-blocking or engulfing banner facilities.
  7. One farm/academy account per alliance.
  8. No going tagless to hit a Top 10 alliance to dodge the NAP.
  9. Event PvP rules override the NAP during SvS, Bear Invasion Alliance, Brothers Battle, etc.
  10. Respect, cooperation, and fair play at all times.

Guidelines

Snow Busters — Strategy Guidelines

Equipment priority. Upgrade the Coat and Gloves first; only proceed to other equipment once these are prioritized.

Stop threshold. Once territory clearance reaches 95%, stop manually clearing snow and use the rocket (instant-clear item) to finish the remainder.

Fuel efficiency. Each furnace upgrade automatically expands your territory. Avoid wasting fuel clearing tiles in a circle around the furnace — advance in a zigzag pattern instead to minimize wasted energy.

Clearing order. Three snow types require different energy amounts: white (lowest), blue (moderate), and ice (highest). Clear them in that order — white, then blue, then ice — since each fireplace upgrade reduces the energy cost per tile going forward.

Guidelines

Twin Stars — Quick Tips

Team: team up with active players. Incomplete teams may be filled automatically, and the team can't be changed after selection ends.

Dice: Normal Dice move a random 1–6 steps. Save Crystal Dice — they let you choose the exact number.

Strategy: save Crystal Dice until you're 1–6 spaces from the Adventure Chest or a valuable reward, then land exactly on it. Don't waste Crystal Dice on random moves!

Heads up

Gen 2 Heroes are coming

Every hero generation brings three new heroes — one Infantry, one Lancer, and one Marksman. Each generation includes 5–6 Lucky Wheels (a generation lasts about 80 days). You'll still have the option to pick previous heroes too.

Flint arrives on the wheel in three days and some hours — an Infantry hero, and an important one to get. He's a massive upgrade over Sergey.

There's no exact number of diamonds you need to get him, but the wheel comes down to luck plus specific spin milestones that guarantee hero shards.

  • 1 spin: 1,500 diamonds — 10 spins: 13,500 diamonds.
  • Use the 10-spin option — it's cheaper per spin.
  • Guaranteed shard-chest milestones will show on the wheel once the event goes live.
  • The last milestone is at 120 spins — don't spin past that unless you're seriously loaded. Reaching 120 spins takes roughly 162,000 diamonds.
  • HOC Day 2 gives points toward the wheel.

Note: don't use general shards on wheel heroes.

Summary:

  • Only spin the wheel if you have enough gems for 70 or 120 total spins per event.
  • Always spin 10x at a time to get the discount.
  • Your goal is to get at least 4 stars on the hero.
  • Never use general shards on a wheel hero — better to save them until you have ~475, then use them all on a non-wheel hero at the start of a future generation to get them to 4 stars.
  • If you participate in HOC, use the wheel on Day 2 too.

Diplomacy

NAP Agreement

Non-aggression pact negotiated between R4/R5 of both alliances. All members must follow these terms:

  1. No attacks on cities, rallies, HQ, or banners of NAP members.
  2. No scouting NAP cities or resource tiles.
  3. No poaching NAP players.
  4. Violations are solved first between R4/R5 of both alliances.
  5. Penalties hit the player only, not the alliance — 1st: warning, 2nd: demotion, 3rd: banned from NAP.
  6. Fortress / Facility / Stronghold: no city hits during these battles; 1 fortress per alliance (by rank, checked Sundays); Stronghold is Top 4 alliances only; no path-blocking or engulfing banner facilities.
  7. One farm/academy account per alliance.
  8. No going tagless to hit a Top 10 alliance to dodge the NAP.
  9. Event PvP rules override the NAP during SvS, Bear Invasion Alliance, Brothers Battle, etc.
  10. Respect, cooperation, and fair play at all times.

Guidelines

Snow Busters — Strategy Guidelines

Equipment priority. Upgrade the Coat and Gloves first; only proceed to other equipment once these are prioritized.

Stop threshold. Once territory clearance reaches 95%, stop manually clearing snow and use the rocket (instant-clear item) to finish the remainder.

Fuel efficiency. Each furnace upgrade automatically expands your territory. Avoid wasting fuel clearing tiles in a circle around the furnace — advance in a zigzag pattern instead to minimize wasted energy.

Clearing order. Three snow types require different energy amounts: white (lowest), blue (moderate), and ice (highest). Clear them in that order — white, then blue, then ice — since each fireplace upgrade reduces the energy cost per tile going forward.

Twin Stars — Quick Tips

Team: team up with active players. Incomplete teams may be filled automatically, and the team can't be changed after selection ends.

Dice: Normal Dice move a random 1–6 steps. Save Crystal Dice — they let you choose the exact number.

Strategy: save Crystal Dice until you're 1–6 spaces from the Adventure Chest or a valuable reward, then land exactly on it. Don't waste Crystal Dice on random moves!

Heads up

Gen 2 Heroes are coming

Every hero generation brings three new heroes — one Infantry, one Lancer, and one Marksman. Each generation includes 5–6 Lucky Wheels (a generation lasts about 80 days). You'll still have the option to pick previous heroes too.

Flint arrives on the wheel in three days and some hours — an Infantry hero, and an important one to get. He's a massive upgrade over Sergey.

There's no exact number of diamonds you need to get him, but the wheel comes down to luck plus specific spin milestones that guarantee hero shards.

  • 1 spin: 1,500 diamonds — 10 spins: 13,500 diamonds.
  • Use the 10-spin option — it's cheaper per spin.
  • Guaranteed shard-chest milestones will show on the wheel once the event goes live.
  • The last milestone is at 120 spins — don't spin past that unless you're seriously loaded. Reaching 120 spins takes roughly 162,000 diamonds.
  • HOC Day 2 gives points toward the wheel.

Note: don't use general shards on wheel heroes.

Summary:

  • Only spin the wheel if you have enough gems for 70 or 120 total spins per event.
  • Always spin 10x at a time to get the discount.
  • Your goal is to get at least 4 stars on the hero.
  • Never use general shards on a wheel hero — better to save them until you have ~475, then use them all on a non-wheel hero at the start of a future generation to get them to 4 stars.
  • If you participate in HOC, use the wheel on Day 2.

Diplomacy

NAP Agreement

Non-aggression pact negotiated between R4/R5 of both alliances. All members must follow these terms:

  1. No attacks on cities, rallies, HQ, or banners of NAP members.
  2. No scouting NAP cities or resource tiles.
  3. No poaching NAP players.
  4. Violations are solved first between R4/R5 of both alliances.
  5. Penalties hit the player only, not the alliance — 1st: warning, 2nd: demotion, 3rd: banned from NAP.
  6. Fortress / Facility / Stronghold: no city hits during these battles; 1 fortress per alliance (by rank, checked Sundays); Stronghold is Top 4 alliances only; no path-blocking or engulfing banner facilities.
  7. One farm/academy account per alliance.
  8. No going tagless to hit a Top 10 alliance to dodge the NAP.
  9. Event PvP rules override the NAP during SvS, Bear Invasion Alliance, Brothers Battle, etc.
  10. Respect, cooperation, and fair play at all times.

Guidelines

Snow Busters — Strategy Guidelines

Equipment priority. Upgrade the Coat and Gloves first; only proceed to other equipment once these are prioritized.

Stop threshold. Once territory clearance reaches 95%, stop manually clearing snow and use the rocket (instant-clear item) to finish the remainder.

Fuel efficiency. Each furnace upgrade automatically expands your territory. Avoid wasting fuel clearing tiles in a circle around the furnace — advance in a zigzag pattern instead to minimize wasted energy.

Clearing order. Three snow types require different energy amounts: white (lowest), blue (moderate), and ice (highest). Clear them in that order — white, then blue, then ice — since each fireplace upgrade reduces the energy cost per tile going forward.

Twin Stars — Quick Tips

Team: team up with active players. Incomplete teams may be filled automatically, and the team can't be changed after selection ends.

Dice: Normal Dice move a random 1–6 steps. Save Crystal Dice — they let you choose the exact number.

Strategy: save Crystal Dice until you're 1–6 spaces from the Adventure Chest or a valuable reward, then land exactly on it. Don't waste Crystal Dice on random moves!
